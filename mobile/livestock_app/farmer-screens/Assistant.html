What is foot-and-mouth disease?

It is a highly contagious viral disease of cloven-hoofed animals, including cattle, goats, sheep and pigs. Outbreaks are managed by the veterinary authority.

Source: World Organisation for Animal Health · [approved source list]

My cow has a fever and stopped eating. What medicine should I give?

I can't recommend medicines or doses, and I can't tell what is wrong with your cow. Fever with loss of appetite can have several causes, and some spread to other animals.
